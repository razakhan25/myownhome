## Plaid (as of Nov 2, 2026)

- New teams get the **Trial plan**: free, real production data, **10 Production Items forever** (slots are never freed, even by `/item/remove`), includes Transactions + Recurring Transactions + Refresh, Balance, Liabilities, Assets, Investments, Auth, Identity; excludes Income. Auto-approved after identity verification (manual review 2–3 business days). OAuth banks (Chase, BofA, Wells Fargo, Capital One, Citi…) work immediately. Support is AI-assistant only. Upgrading to Production is one-way.
- "Limited Production" closed to new US signups on April 15, 2026 (the public pricing page still describes it).
- **Production** = Pay-as-you-go (no minimum) or Growth (annual commitment): apply in the Dashboard (business details, products + use cases, beneficial owner, plan + billing); most applications auto-reviewed in a couple of business days. Then the security questionnaire unlocks (needed for Chase and other OAuth banks on paid plans; submit early).
- Billing basis: Transactions, Recurring, Liabilities, Investments = per Item per month (not pro-rated, as long as an access_token exists → call `/item/remove` on unlink); Balance = per request; Assets = per request, by Items and days; `/item/*` and `/accounts/get` free. Dollar prices only appear inside the Production application flow.
- `/transactions/sync` is the recommended endpoint (`days_requested` default 90, max 730); listen for `SYNC_UPDATES_AVAILABLE`. `/transactions/recurring/get` is a Transactions add-on (bundled on Trial; request it as a product on paid plans). Sandbox test user `user_transactions_dynamic` has six months of recurring data.
- Node SDK `plaid` 50.0.0 (Nov 2, 2026; a major bump every few weeks), `react-plaid-link` 5.0.0.
